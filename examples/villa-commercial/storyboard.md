# Villa Saadiyat — Where the Light Lives

**Format:** 16:9 · 40 s · 30 fps · domain: architecture

## Logline

A day of light moves through a modern Abu Dhabi villa, from the dunes at golden hour to a quiet terrace at dusk.

## Creative concept

The villa is presented through light, not features. Every shot follows the same low golden sun as it travels from the coast, across the façade and through the house, ending as the interior lights take over at dusk. The motif is a slow reveal from behind a natural layer: dune grass, palm fronds, a timber screen.

## Visual direction

Calm, architectural, unhurried. One slow camera move per shot, corrected verticals, 24mm tilt-shift for exteriors and 35mm for interiors. Warm golden highlights over clean neutral midtones, with travertine, timber and bronze glass as the hero materials. No fast cuts and no lens flares beyond natural sun glints. People appear small and unposed.

## Continuity bible

- **Style:** photorealistic high-end architectural photography
- **Grade:** warm golden highlights, clean neutral midtones, cool sky gradient, gentle contrast
- **Lens package:** 24mm tilt-shift lens, f/8, straight converging-free verticals
- **Lighting:** golden hour sun low from the west raking across the façade, warm interior lights glowing, soft sky fill
- **Palette:** travertine beige, sand gold, bronze glass, deep sea teal, warm white interior light
- **@guest** (character): a woman in her thirties in a loose ivory linen dress, barefoot, seen at a distance, never facing the camera
- **@villa** (location): a two-storey contemporary villa of honed beige travertine and board-formed white concrete, with full-height bronze-tinted glazing, a cantilevered roof slab and a 25 m infinity pool facing the sea, on the Saadiyat Island coast
